    The rules

    You get 100 tries a day and no clock runs out. A wrong answer uses a try and breaks your chain. Screen time goes in your bank. You can cash in up to 45 minutes on a school day and 90 on a weekend day. Anything left over stays in the bank, so save up during the week for a big weekend.

    Example: a Lightning answer on a 12 chain is (16 + 8) × 1.5 = 36 seconds. A really great day earns about an hour.
